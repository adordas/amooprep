# AmooPrep Refund Policy — Draft

**Recommended starting policy:** Requests made within 7 days of the initial purchase may be reviewed for a refund. Refunds may be declined in cases of fraud, chargeback abuse, or repeated purchase/refund patterns.

Before publishing, decide whether you want to keep the 7-day window or use a different period, then make the same policy visible on your Lemon Squeezy checkout/store and your public policy page.
