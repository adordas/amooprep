Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

AmooPrep is designed to minimize data collection. This policy describes the planned public Chrome extension and this static website.

Website

This website does not intentionally use advertising trackers, analytics cookies, user accounts, or behavioral profiling. If the hosting provider records basic server logs for security and reliability, those logs are controlled by the hosting provider.

Study data stored on your device

The AmooPrep Chrome extension stores study progress, question statistics, mastery information, preferences, and Premium license state locally using Chrome extension storage. This information is used to provide study features and remember your progress on that Chrome profile.

Premium license activation

When you choose to activate Premium, the extension may send the license key and an installation/instance identifier to Lemon Squeezy's licensing service over HTTPS as needed to activate, validate, or deactivate a license. AmooPrep stores the license key and license instance information locally so Premium status can be remembered and checked.

Payments

Premium payments are completed through Lemon Squeezy's hosted checkout. AmooPrep does not receive or store your full payment card number. Lemon Squeezy processes checkout and transaction data under its own privacy and payment policies.

Data AmooPrep does not request for study features

AmooPrep does not need your browsing history, school credentials, live exam content, proctoring data, advertising identifier, precise location, contacts, microphone, or camera to provide its study features.

Academic use

AmooPrep is intended for preparation before assessments. The extension is not designed to read, answer, inject into, or automate live exams or proctoring systems.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, use the AmooPrep contact page. A public support email can be added before commercial launch.
