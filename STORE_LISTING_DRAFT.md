# Chrome Web Store listing draft

## Name
AmooPrep — Adaptive Study Companion

## Short description
Adaptive practice, mock exams, flashcards, and mastery tracking for focused pre-exam study.

## Single purpose
AmooPrep is a pre-exam study companion that helps learners practice course concepts, identify weak areas, and improve recall through adaptive review.

## Long description
Study smarter with adaptive practice that responds to your mistakes. AmooPrep combines practice questions, alternate-wording retries, flashcards, mock exams, topic mastery, timelines, people/idea review, weak-area targeting, and local progress tracking.

The free edition includes a useful starter set. Optional Premium course packs unlock larger question banks, complete flashcard decks, and full-length mock exams. Payment is processed by Lemon Squeezy. AmooPrep—not Google—is the seller.

AmooPrep is an independent educational product and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by any university, school, testing provider, or proctoring service. It is designed for study before assessments and does not read or interact with live exams or proctoring systems.
