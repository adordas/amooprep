Refund Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

We want purchasers to have a fair opportunity to confirm that a Premium course pack works for their needs.

Seven-day request window

Refund requests submitted within 7 calendar days of the initial purchase may be reviewed for a refund. Where applicable, refunds are processed through Lemon Squeezy, the Merchant of Record for AmooPrep Premium purchases.

Possible exclusions

A refund may be declined where there is evidence of fraud, chargeback abuse, repeated purchase/refund behavior, license-key resale or redistribution, or other material abuse of the product or payment system.

Technical problems

If Premium access does not activate correctly, contact support first so we can attempt to resolve the licensing issue. A technical problem that cannot reasonably be resolved may be considered when reviewing a refund request.

How to request a refund

Use the AmooPrep support page and include the email used for purchase and enough transaction information to identify the order. Do not send full payment-card details.
